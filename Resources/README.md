# Product artwork

`M230II.png` is intentionally not tracked. `scripts/fetch-assets.sh` downloads the
wood-finish M230 II product image from [Edifier's official product page](https://www.edifier.com/cn/product/product-942.html)
when building the app. The original image is unchanged; the native view draws its
subject region as a thumbnail.

Product imagery, Edifier's name and trademarks belong to their respective owners
and are not covered by this repository's MIT license. The image is optional at
runtime; battery/EQ controls work without it.
